# 2. Story

What is the beginning?

Answer:

> The player awakens inside the inactive GTV Network after receiving a mysterious digital signal. The Core Terminal identifies the player as the new Digital Explorer and requests help restoring the network.

---

What is the conflict?

Answer:

> The GTV Network has lost power, causing its locations, systems, and knowledge archives to become inaccessible. The player must reactivate each sector by completing missions and restoring the Core Network.

---

What is the ending?

Answer:

> After restoring every sector and completing the final mission, the GTV Network returns online. The player is officially recognized as a GTV Digital Explorer, unlocking the final credits and celebrating the completion of the journey.
